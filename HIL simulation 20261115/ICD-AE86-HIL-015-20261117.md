# Hardware Interface Control Document (ICD) & Physical Bench Spec
**Project:** 1986 Toyota Corolla AE86 (4A-GE 16V TCCS) Non-Invasive HIL & Cockpit FDR Suite  
**Document ID:** `ICD-AE86-HIL-015` | **Design Standard:** Non-Invasive Observation (`< 0.05 mA` Parasitic Load)

---

## 1. System Boundary & Grounding Philosophy
To guarantee zero interference with the factory 1986 Toyota Computer Controlled System (TCCS) ECU and prevent ignition ground loops:
1. **Single-Point Chassis/ECU Ground (`E1`):** All analog buffer reference grounds tie exclusively to the TCCS ECU sensor ground (`E1`), never to alternator or starter chassis bolts.
2. **Galvanic Isolation Barrier:** High-energy ignition primary lines (`IG-`) and 12V inductive relay coils (`T-VIS VSV`, `Radiator Fan Relay`) are isolated from the 3.3V MCU logic domain via optical isolators and low-side logic-level MOSFET drivers.
3. **Automotive Load-Dump Power Conditioning (ISO 7637-2):** The cockpit FDR power supply takes `12V–14.4V` Ignition-Switched (`IG+`) power through a `1A` polyfuse, `1N5822` reverse-polarity Schottky diode, `SMBJ18CA` bidirectional TVS transient suppressor, and an automotive-grade `LM2940-5.0` low-dropout regulator (`45V` load-dump rated).

## 3. Schematic Architecture Topologies

### Topology A: Non-Invasive High-Impedance Analog Buffer (`VTA`, `THW`, `OX`, `VF`)
```text
TCCS Harness Tap ---> [100 kΩ, 1/4W] --->+---> [MCP6004 Op-Amp (+)] ---> [100 Ω] ---> MCU #2 ADC (A0-A3)
                                         |           |___(-)___|               |
                                      [BAT54S]     (Unity-Gain Follower)     [100 nF]
                                  (3.3V/5V & GND)                              |
                                                                              GND
```

### Topology B: Galvanically Isolated Ignition Pulse Interface (IG- & IGf)
```text
TCCS IG- (400V Spike) ---> [10 kΩ, 1W] --->+---> [6N137 Anode (Pin 2)]
                                           |
                                        [1N4007] (Reverse Spike Shunt)
                                           |
TCCS Ground (E1) --------------------------+---> [6N137 Cathode (Pin 3)]
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------- (Optical Isolation Barrier)
MCU #2 +3.3V/+5V ----------> [4.7 kΩ] ---------> [6N137 Output (Pin 6)] --------> MCU #2 D2 (INT0)
MCU #2 GND ------------------------------------> [6N137 GND (Pin 5)]
```

### Topology C: Protected Low-Side Inductive Actuator Driver (T-VIS & Radiator Fan)
```text
                                          +12V Switched Ignition (Fused)
                                                       |
                                              +--------+--------+
                                              |                 |
                                        [Relay / VSV]      [1N4007] (Cathode to +12V)
                                              |                 |
                                              +--------+--------+
                                                       |
MCU #2 D8/D9 ---> [100 Ω] --->+---> [Gate]      [Drain] (IRLZ44N N-Channel MOSFET)
                              |                 [Source] ---> Chassis Ground
                           [10 kΩ]
                              |
                             GND
```
